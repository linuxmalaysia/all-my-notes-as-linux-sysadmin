## Critical Assumptions & Planning Proposal Purpose

> **IMPORTANT: Educational and Planning Context**

All costs, designs, unit amounts, and scenarios detailed within this document and throughout the entire repository are based entirely on assumptions. 

This project and its accompanying documentation are compiled strictly for **training, educational, and planning proposal purposes**. The scenarios discussed, including architectural designs, financial estimations, and infrastructure configurations, are hypothetical blueprints used to map Linux System Administration knowledge to the National Occupational Skills Standard (NOSS) framework.
