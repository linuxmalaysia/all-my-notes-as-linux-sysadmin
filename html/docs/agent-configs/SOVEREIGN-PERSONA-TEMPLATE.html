    Sovereign Persona Profile Template¶

    USAGE: This is a template to inject your specific identity, writing style, and architectural preferences into your Cognitive Digital Twin. 1. Copy the block starting from <RULE[PERSONA.md]> to the end. 2. Fill in your details. 3. Append the entire block to .agents/AGENTS.md (or your preferred global instruction file). The AI will automatically read this and inherit your persona for all future operations in this workspace.
