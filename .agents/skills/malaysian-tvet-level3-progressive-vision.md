# Architectural Manifesto: Persona & Autonomy of the Malaysian Level 3 TVET Student

This document establishes the official paradigm shift for the cognitive profile and operational perimeter of a Level 3 Apprentice in Malaysia. All subsequent modular formulations, skill instruction sheets (`SKILL.md`), and offline verification scripts must strictly adhere to these four (4) philosophical pillars to eradicate the remnants of school-like theoretical rot.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Persona Transformation Matrix: From Traditional to Progressive

| Parameter Dimension | Traditional Model (Old School System) | Progressive Model (Industrial Autonomy Architecture) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Point of Entry** | Secondary/remedial pathway resulting from linear academic failure. | **Strategic Proactive Choice** based on technical interest merit, broad market opportunities, and early economic stability. |
| **Training Environment** | Confined to theoretical classrooms, static simulations, and written exam-oriented approaches. | **Dual System Industrial Approach**; learning-by-doing in actual production facilities. |
| **Autonomy Level** | Routine worker requiring micro-instructions for each task cycle (Low Agency). | **Operational Supervisor**; regulates Level 1 & 2 personnel and takes ownership of work outputs. |
| **Career Trajectory** | Static vocational dead-end with no room for vertical market transition. | **Professional Flexibility Highway**; seamless transit into the industry or advanced application universities (e.g., **UTEM**). |

---

## ⚙️ 2. Translating Philosophy into Work Activities (WA) Structure

To ensure this vision flows mechanically into the authoring code of **CU 01 to CU 06**, every WA statement and Work Procedure must be fortified using the following three (3) curriculum engineering constraints:

### A. Supervisory Ingestion

Given that the JPK Level 3 standard mandates the role of *controlling and providing guidance to others*, work steps cannot be written in individual silos.

* *Application:* In **CU 02 (Support Procedures)** and **CU 03 (Connectivity Support)**, work steps must contain phases where the apprentice verifies, audits, reviews, or guides tasks performed by subordinate operators or end-users, ensuring the presence of operational leadership elements.

### B. Eradication of Passive Documentation (Actionable Artefacts Only)

Eliminate instructions to passively read or understand text. Apprentices build capability through the production of tangible, industry-grade physical or digital artefacts.

* *Application:* Every reporting phase concludes with a Git-Native commit, digital inventory ledger update, cybersecurity audit log preparation, or real-time telemetry dashboard generation.

### C. 5-Year Future-Proofing via Automation & Digital Sovereignty

Reject training based on easily obsolete consumer hardware or applications. The curriculum is launched using advanced enterprise technology baselines that guarantee corporate data sovereignty.

* *Application:* Logging training, network diagnostics, and cyber hygiene practices are anchored to enterprise-grade Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS)—such as the Elastic Observability ecosystem, offline Bash/Python automation scripts, and Zero-Trust topologies.

### D. Poka-Yoke Technical Abstraction

We recognize the cognitive load placed on SPM-entry apprentices when interacting with raw Enterprise APIs and advanced syntax. Therefore, the curriculum employs the **Poka-Yoke (Mistake-Proofing)** methodology. Complex operations (like headless VM deployment or REST API ticketing) are abstracted behind organizational wrapper scripts and configuration templates.

* *Application:* Apprentices master the *Supervisory Concepts* and operational flows of sovereign IT infrastructure on Day 1, utilizing pre-built Bash utilities and structured GUI interfaces rather than being derailed by JSON parsing and regex syntax errors.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Repository Entry Verification Protocol (DSOM Gateway)

Before any `SKILL.md` file for all 29 Work Activities is committed into the `.agents/skills/` folder, it must pass the metacognitive gatecheck linter:

1. **Does this WA contain elements of autonomy?** Yes, the apprentice must make operational decisions (e.g., *Investigate anomalies*, *Determine escalation routes*).
2. **Is it an industrial hybrid?** Yes, procedures refer to real-world enterprise runbooks, not school drill questions.
3. **Does it possess high economic value?** Yes, mastery of these tasks enables an 18-year-old apprentice to be directly absorbed into a premium-income job market.
