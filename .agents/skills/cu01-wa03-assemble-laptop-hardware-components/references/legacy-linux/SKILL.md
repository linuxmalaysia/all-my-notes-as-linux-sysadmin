# Work Activity Summary
This skill governs the execution of ASSEMBLE LAPTOP HARDWARE COMPONENTS to produce functional operational outputs according to enterprise quality standards.

## Required Utilities & Prerequisites
*   **System Commands:** standard linux utilities
*   **Hardware / Equipment:** Laptops (1:1), Desktops (1:1)
*   **Tools:** Screwdrivers (Philips & Flathead), Crimping tools, Punch down tools, Pliers and pry tools, Anti-static wrist straps, ESD mats, Multimeters, Cable testers
*   **Materials:** RJ45 connectors, Cat5e/Cat6 cables, Cable ties, Labels, Thermal paste
*   **Documentation:** organizational SOPs, vendor manuals

## Core Execution Steps (Work Steps)
*   **Step 3.1:** Wear designated PPE.
*   **Step 3.2:** Prepare laptop chassis.
*   **Step 3.3:** Assemble internal components.
*   **Step 3.4:** Connect internal cables.
*   **Step 3.5:** Secure laptop casing.
*   **Step 3.6:** Verify hardware detection.

## Performance Criteria (Quality Gates)
> Note: For full compliance checklists, refer to the Compliance archive.

*   Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) safety tools applied.
*   Laptop chassis physical postures and internal battery power modules equipped.
*   Compact internal components (SODIMM memory, NVMe/M.2 storage, and wireless network cards) assembled.
*   Internal flexible ribbon cables and integrated display assemblies connected to the system board.
*   Laptop outer casings and base cover mounting screws secured.
*   Initial power-on (POST) and hardware component detection confirmed.
